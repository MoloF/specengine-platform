# The Flow screen: layout and forms

How a flow read is placed and how its edges are drawn; `flow-canvas`, `flow-scenario`, `handoff-progress` (left to right again, the owner's look closing 08's v5 Q14), `room-and-scroll` (the room, the selection's look), `canvas-clicks` (Clicks on Graph and the Chain too), code `ui/src/flow/layout.ts`, `geometry.ts`, `FlowCanvas.tsx`, `FlowLegend.tsx`, `labels.ts`. The screen around it: `spec-flow-screen.md`.

## Layout

`layout.ts` `layoutFlow(view)`, hand-written and pure: a read in; absolute boxes, its lane, stage dividers and edges out, inputs sorted first; one read, one lane. Only the read's nodes are drawn: the drawing reads are cut to the flow (`spec-flow-cuts.md`).

- **Layer.** The box's `distance` in the read drawing it: a folded node carries the least of what it holds. A scope's walk restarts at 0.
- **Columns.** Left to right, one column per distinct distance; one distance shares one x. In a column boxes run top to bottom, ordered by four barycentre sweeps, then eight passes align each column with its neighbours. A forward edge skipping a column gets a passage slot there, so it passes no box. `COLUMN_GAP` 96 between columns: a pitch of 256 px, 235.5 on screen at 0.92. An edge's words stay in its gap, at most `LABEL_WIDTH` 88 px (`--flow-edge-label-width` 5.5rem, the gap less 4 px each side), one word a line, two lines, then an ellipsis; a word past about 12 characters is cut there, whole in the List, the panel and the arrow's name. An edge within a column labels in the gap right of it. `BOX_GAP` 48 between a column's boxes (more under a loop), `LANE_PAD` 16. A box is 160 px wide (`BOX_WIDTH`) and 104 tall (`BOX_HEIGHT`), padded 8 and 12 px, its title alone at 14 px, up to two lines. It is keyed by path and line; a second holder of an ID gets its own id, and links land on the first.
- **Stage dividers.** A vertical rule, a third node type, `aria-hidden`, never focusable, taking no pointer, stands left of the first column holding a box of a stage, between it and the previous column (`FlowDivider` `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`): from the top of a words strip `DIVIDER_HEIGHT` 40 above the walk to below the lowest arc track. Its words stand at its top beside the rule, in the column's width, two lines, then an ellipsis. Stages beginning in one column share one, their raw words joined ` · `. A picture of fewer than two stages, a stage view among them, draws none; a band box opens none. A box's accessible name adds its whole stage.
- **Routes.** Columns decide, never a type or `kind`: forward, S-curves out of a box's right side into the next one's left, in the gaps; against the walk, a repeat among them, an orthogonal arc on its own track below the walk, the shorter nearer, leaving and entering by a box's bottom when it is its column's lowest, else by the gap beside it; same column beside; to itself a loop under the box; to or from the band, by a trunk over it to the band box's gutter. No arc or band edge crosses a box. Ports spread along a side; labels stack.
- **The band.** `distance` null: definitions, unreached steps, a part linked to the flow that no step of this picture reaches, children entered from outside, whom the read's own walk does not reach, so the caption is true to the read. Below the lowest arc track and the trunks, captioned `flow.offWalk` with its count and `flow.offWalkAbout` (at least 320 px, `BAND_CAPTION_WIDTH`, the caption clamped to it), wrapped at the walk's width or four boxes, whichever is wider; edges to one band box share a trunk by (target, type, kind); linked neighbours take a direct line.
- **Off the walk.** An edge with an end whose `distance` is null is drawn only while one of its ends, or the edge itself, is selected (the List, a gap item and the hash select too); Esc's clear hides it again.
- **Stacking and view.** Node types: a box, the band's caption, a divider; stacking is manual, edges under boxes. A picture fits once, when its read lands; then only the owner, `c` and a gap activation move it. The fit (`fitOf`) takes the lesser of the two sides' ratios, at most 100 %, at least `FLOW_FIT_MIN_ZOOM` 0.92, so a title renders at 12.9 px or more, centred on a side it fits, at the margin on one it overflows: a long walk lands at 0.92 from its first column and is panned, never shrunk. A pinch goes down to `FLOW_PINCH_MIN_ZOOM` 0.5; Graph keeps its own 0.6 and 0.1. The canvas fills the studio's centre pane; the wheel pans it, a pinch or the zoom key zooms it, and a selection moves nothing; the minimap, bottom-left, shows only while the picture is not whole in view.
- **Clicks**, on every canvas. A press moving at most `CLICK_DISTANCE` 5 px before its release is a click (`paneClickDistance`); past it, a pan that selects nothing. The one constant is `ui/src/graph/geometry.ts`'s, passed by Flow's `FlowCanvas`, Graph's `GraphCanvas` and Roadmap's `RoadmapChain`. Boxes take no `nopan`, so a drag starting on one pans. No minimap pans, zooms or takes the pointer (`.graph-canvas .react-flow__minimap` `pointer-events: none`, every canvas's wrapper carrying `.graph-canvas`; Flow's own `.flow-canvas` declaration kept; the Chain draws none), nor do Flow's words-only nodes, dividers and band caption (`WORDS_ONLY`'s inline `pointer-events: none` over xyflow's `all`): a box under them takes its click. A press moves nothing: Flow's and Graph's `onFocus` centres a box not wholly in view only when its focus is `:focus-visible` (Tab, the arrows, the first box after Flow's Space into a group); a mouse, touch or pen press never moves the view, Graph's still roving its tab stop. The Chain's boxes take no focus.

## Forms

`labels.ts` alone tells gap states, codes, rollups and `edge.kind` apart; an unknown rollup is a neutral badge. `FlowEdge` needs no type word: `from` and `to` are in reading direction, and rows and arcs ask no type.

- **Type.** Colour `--flow-edge-0` to `-3` and plain dash go by place in the served `types` (`typesOf(view)`), so a type keeps one look in every picture; a type missing from the list takes the first. One exception: a lit edge ("Selection") takes the accent, so its type shows by its dash alone; the List, the panel and the arrow's name still say it.
- **Kind.** `FLOW_DASHES`, seven distinct: four by a type's place, of which a read fills three, three by `kind`; `repeat` alone is dotted, in every project, and heavier. The closed kinds `alternative`, `parallel`, `repeat` are SpecEngine's words, shown through `flow.edgeKind.*` on the canvas, the List, the legend, the panel and accessible names; an unknown value shows raw, with its type's dash.
- **Label.** On the canvas, the kind and `when` (clipped at 24 characters), no count, in the gap ("Columns"); the List keeps `when` whole and `×<count>`, the edge panel the count. `FlowLegend` prints each sample, the type raw, the arc, "runs against the walk", and after it the selection's row ("Selection"), its about text the left-to-right walk and the off-walk rule; `flow.canvasHint` says the walk runs left to right.
- **Boxes.** A plain box rounds at `--radius-m` 8 px. Start and end boxes round at `--radius-xl` 16 px and keep the box's padding, so no corner cuts a row (at 104 px an end box's 80 px inside takes its three rows, centred into the padding); a start keeps its 2 px solid accent border, an end its 4 px double one, and both their Start and End marks: shape, border and words say it without colour.
- **Selection.** While a box is selected, every drawn edge at it is lit: in, out, off the walk, its own loop; while an arrow is selected, it alone is, whatever selected it (a click, the List, a gap item, the hash). `edgeOf` sets a lit edge's style inline, since a type's inline colour wins over any stylesheet rule: `stroke` and its marker `var(--graph-edge-emphasis)`, `strokeWidth` `var(--graph-edge-width-emphasis)`, over a repeat's width too, its dash kept; in and out take one accent; its label `is-emphasis`. While any edge is lit, every other drawn edge's group takes `is-faded`, `opacity: var(--flow-edge-faded-opacity)` 0.4 on path and marker, its type's colour kept, and its label `is-faded`, `--text-subtlest`, never an opacity. With nothing lit (no selection, Esc, a selected box with no drawn edge) nothing fades. Boxes, the band's caption and dividers never fade or dim (`styles.test.ts` ST-6, narrowed by name). Lit edges come last in the edges array: at the same `zIndex` 1 they paint over faded ones on a shared trunk, under the boxes' 2. The fade's rules sit under `.flow-canvas` or on `.flow-edge-label`, never `.graph-canvas`, which Flow's wrapper also carries; Graph's older `.graph-canvas .react-flow__edge.is-emphasis` rule still matches a lit Flow edge, with the same values, and the inline style wins. Graph and Roadmap are unchanged. The legend's row `flow.legend.selected`, a sample line at the accent and its width, says it in words: a selected box's lines in and out, or a selected line; the others fade.
- **Names.** An arrow's accessible name is `flow.edgeName` "From <from> to <to>", then the type raw, the kind (`flow.edgeKind.*`) and `when`, clipped as on the canvas; the ends are their boxes' words (`boxWords`: the title, else the name). A List row is `flow.edgeRow` "to <to>", then the type, kind, `when <when>` and `×<count>` (`edgeRowWords`). The type is quoted nowhere (`policy.test.ts`).

## Known limits

- A band edge's gutter line crosses the band's caption.
- A mouse Close of Graph's details focuses its box from script, not `:focus-visible`, so a box out of view is not centred; Flow's panel does the same.
- **Columns in view.** At 1440 with both panes the canvas is 567 px: about 2.2 pitches at 0.92, two columns whole and a third begun, the trade-off for the room the owner asked for. More needs narrower boxes, a lower floor or a collapsible pane: the owner's call.
- Faded lines fall under 3:1 against the canvas by design: the lit ones carry the meaning, faded words stay at `--text-subtlest`. In dark the accent sits near `--flow-edge-0`; its width and the fade tell them apart.
- A selected end box loses its double border to the selection's 2 px; its End mark and the Selected tab still say it.
- The columns in view, the dividers' words, the terminators' rows inside their borders and the lit lines are proven by looks in Chrome; tests pin the pitch, no label over a box, the inline accent and the fade.
