# department-practices (Phase 7, pool): each department's standing rules, fed to its cards and tasks

This item comes from the owner's word of 2026-11-19, wish 2 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-19"). Each department keeps a corner of documents holding its agreed rules. Work and review cite those rules by a direct, current link.

**Intent:**
- A typed `disciplines:` key names `[[card.flow]] disciplines` words. Any document carrying it is a practice, and its rules are its `{#ID}` sections.
- `get_card`'s context and `get_task` carry the practices of the card's discipline. They come after the card's own targets and criteria, within `bundle_task`, and degrade to rule IDs.
- The card modal lists them, and rule IDs cited in card text become links.
- `spec check` warns about a discipline no flow lists.
- English example practices per trade go under `scripts/presets/`, read by the guards `no_domain.rs`, `workspace_names.rs` and `anonymity.rs` (LG-8).

**Needs a new ADR:** a document that names disciplines becomes standing context for their cards and tasks, and gates nothing. It extends ADR-0027, amends the bundle's rule "by link type, direction and `[ids]` scope only, never a kind" (`spec-cli-bundle.md` "Layers"), and touches ADR-0056, ADR-0073 and ADR-0012.

**Working answers**, the owner's to revise:
- anyone proposes, a lead confirms inside its `[zones]`, and the owner confirms anywhere;
- `[auto]` never applies a practice edit;
- a `T-` task no card lists gets none.

**Out:** any gate, verdict or compliance score (ADR-0012), and entitlements beyond the leads' zones. The `get_card` and `get_task` descriptions change, so the plugin version does; `HUB_TOOLS` does not. Its spec is written in a later run.
