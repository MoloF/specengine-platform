# card-questions (Phase 7, v5 step 5): a task asks the writers, and the answer is a recorded decision

Shipped 2026-11-11 (`docs/features/card-questions.md`; ADR-0077, which supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0057, ADR-0074 and ADR-0075; `docs/canon/card-questions.md`). Development sends a task back with a question, the answer becomes a recorded decision, and who decided what, and why, stays visible (Q5, Q11). Plugin 0.36.0; first-load JS 840.79 kB of 845.

- **The link.** A question, discrepancy or decision names one `card_id`, set when raised, never changed: queue schema 11 and backup format 5, which imports 4.
- **A decision's `why`**, at most 2048 bytes, shown by a record through the opt-in `{{why}}` slot. A record names no card; the card reads its items back.
- **The card read** serves `items`, and its history their events; the work item's **Activity** reads as one thread.
- **The page asks.** Return may ask a question with the move; Ask a question… and Record a decision… on any card with a target; Answer… stages, confirmed on a terminal or by a hub's Confirm. `POST .../cards/:id/items` is a hub's sixth plain write; a stage carries `as`.
- **MCP.** `ask_question`, `report_discrepancy`, `report_decision` take `card_id`, `report_decision` `why`.
- **Rollout.** Older binaries refuse the queue and the dump: every terminal, plugin and hub upgrades from one commit, a hub after a snapshot.
- The cut line was not needed: the UI shipped whole.
