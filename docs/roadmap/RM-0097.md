# card-questions (Phase 7, v5 step 5): a task asks the writers, and the answer is a recorded decision

An ADR `card-questions`, issued with this slice's spec, supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0075. It widens `architecture.md#ui` by one write. The owner's pipeline: development sends a task back with a question, the writers answer, and the answer becomes a decision record or a document that proves the work. Who decided what, and why, stays visible (Q5, Q11). 10-14 pipeline hours.

- **The link.** Question, decision and discrepancy intake items name a `card_id`. This takes queue schema step 10 to 11 and backup format 5, which still imports 4.
- **Decisions** gain a `why` of at most 2048 bytes. A decision record names no card (Q11); the card links back through `record_id`.
- **The card read** lists its items. The task's **Activity** reads as one thread: return, question, decision, move again.
- **Ask the writers.** In the Return dialog, it makes two writes: the move with its reason, and a question naming the card. The writers answer from the card.
- **MCP.** A developer who decides alone files `report_decision` with the card.
- **Hub.** The hub's fence lists the new route as a plain write.
