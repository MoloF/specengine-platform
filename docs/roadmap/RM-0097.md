# card-questions (Phase 7, v5 step 5): a task asks the writers, and the answer is a recorded decision

The spec is `docs/features/card-questions.md` (draft, 2026-11-11, 25 criteria), with ADR-0077 `card-questions`, which supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0057, ADR-0074 and ADR-0075 (`docs/canon/card-questions.md` "The rule") and widens `architecture.md#ui` by a card's questions and decisions. The owner's pipeline: development sends a task back with a question, the writers answer, and the answer becomes a decision record or a document that proves the work. Who decided what, and why, stays visible (Q5, Q11). 13-19 pipeline hours: the Rust lane 7-10, the UI lane 6-9, in parallel.

- **The link.** Question, decision and discrepancy intake items name a `card_id`. This takes queue schema step 10 to 11 and backup format 5, which still imports 4.
- **Decisions** gain a `why` of at most 2048 bytes, which a record shows through an opt-in `{{why}}` slot. A decision record names no card (Q11); the card links back through `record_id`.
- **The card read** lists its items, and its history their events. The task's **Activity** reads as one thread: return, question, decision, move again.
- **Ask a question.** In the Return dialog it makes two writes: the move with its reason, then a question naming the card. The card also asks or records a decision on its own, and an open item is answered from it by a stage.
- **MCP.** A developer who decides alone files `report_decision` with the card; plugin 0.36.0.
- **Hub.** The hub's fence lists the new route as a plain write; an answer is a stage, confirmed as any.
- **Cut line.** If the UI lane runs out, the UI goes to a new plan item `card-questions-ui`, the next free `RM-` number, made at ship only then.
