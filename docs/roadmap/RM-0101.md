# gap-titles (Phase 7, v5 step 3): gap items serve their members' titles

Shipped 2026-11-11 in RM-0095 `doc-modal`'s slice (`docs/features/doc-modal.md`, D5, DM-3, DM-15), the same plugin MINOR, 0.34.0. A follow-up of RM-0094 (`docs/features/flow-scenario.md`, its departure 14): Flow's Gaps control read the whole tree once to title a member folded into a group, one O(N) read at 100k.

- **The read.** Each gap row serves `titles`, parallel to `items`, each member's title or `null`, cut with `items`; the headless `no-start` row is titled by its first ROOT holder, `[[null]]` at Top. The text is unchanged (`docs/canon/spec-flow.md` "Output").
- **The screen** titles every member from the read; Flow's one tree read left is the seek's (`docs/canon/spec-flow-bar.md` "Gaps").
