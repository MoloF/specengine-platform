# edge-table (Phase 7, step 12c): resolved edges kept in the index, incrementally

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/edge-table.md`, `docs/canon/spec-index-edges.md`), the third of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices. The split and D1-D10 are the orchestrator's and await the owner's review.

- The index stores core's resolution of every file's links in four tables (`INDEX_FORMAT` 16), kept inside the update's transaction. A full pass runs when the stamp, the worktree, `[ids]` or the new `[paths]` fingerprint changes; otherwise an incremental pass re-resolves the files whose recorded lookups meet a changed file's names, closed by a fixpoint.
- After any sequence of edits the tables dump as a rebuild's, and they equal core's resolution on every fixture and on RM-0077's 1 000-node corpus.
- `parent:` keeps its qualifiers from the parse, so an escaped `parent:` resolves; `update_paths` probes its paths in one batch.
- Reads do not change yet (RM-0079), and there is no ADR. Review gave REWORK on iteration 1 and ACCEPT on iteration 2; 23 of 23 mutations were killed.

Limits awaiting the owner: no `catch_unwind` around resolution, the fan-out of a file citing every document (RM-0054 measures it), and a probe memo keyed by spelling.
