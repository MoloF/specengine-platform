# hub-workspace (Phase 7, v4, later): a hub's per-login default workspace

On a hub, a login opens on a default workspace named in the workspace table: a presentation-only default, never an entitlement (Q6). `[team]` and its roles stay what they are, the acts' entitlements (ADR-0060), and are untouched. "View as" beside "Acting as" must not read as an identity. It may issue `hub-workspace`, which supplements ADR-0060. Comes after the owner's gate 11b-3. 1-2 pipeline hours.
