# store-reads (Phase 7, step 12d): `show`, `--links` and `bundle` read a subset from the index

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/store-reads.md`, `docs/canon/spec-read-subset.md`), the fourth of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices. The split, T3, T5, Q7 and D1-D11 are the orchestrator's and await the owner's review.

- `spec show`, `show --links` and `spec bundle`, with their MCP and daemon twins, read only the index rows and files around their REFs, from one snapshot per attempt (T3). Every file used is checked against its row's BLAKE3 (T5): a mismatch updates exactly those paths and retries once, and a second mismatch, a missing index or a corrupted row falls back to the whole read.
- Whole-corpus reads keep a file's indexed parse when its bytes match its row (Q7), and stay O(N). Code-layer reads stay whole.
- Answers are byte-equal to the parent commit's on 2 322 golden rows over 19 fixtures and on the 1k `scale` tree. At 100 000 nodes `get_node_links` fell from 1.56 s to 373 ms and the bundle from 1.53 s to 376 ms, about `update_us`, the freshness step that RM-0054 replaces.
- No ADR. Review gave ACCEPT on iterations 1 and 2; 20 of 22 named mutations were killed, the two survivors unreachable through a CLI REF.

Limits awaiting the owner: a subset read cannot see an edit made after its freshness step outside its subset (RM-0054's window); corrupted edge rows under intact file hashes are caught only when a row names no node of the subset.
