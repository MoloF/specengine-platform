# doc-modal (Phase 7, v5 step 3): one document modal everywhere, with the path as its breadcrumb

This is Flow v2's step 3d, together with RM-0091 `english-polish` and RM-0101 `gap-titles`. The owner's words (2026-11-10): the documents in Flow's right panel are unreadable, so he wants one modal that holds everything, a minimal panel, and technical fields out of the way. 8-12 pipeline hours, plus 2-4 for RM-0101. Spec: `docs/features/doc-modal.md` (draft, 2026-11-10, DM-1 to DM-22).

- **The reads** serve each link end's `title` and `end_kind` in `show --links` and `get_node` (`kind` is a labelled edge's already), and each gap row's `titles` (RM-0101); plugin 0.34.0.
- **The modal** works over any screen, with a page at `#/<p>/tree/<REF>/page`. It is RM-0093's work-item layout:
  - the breadcrumb from RM-0094's served path, `ancestors`;
  - the whole text at reading width;
  - links by title, cards and "Inside" (children by `parent:`, by section then raw kind, read on demand), which holds the node's records;
  - technical details folded: ID, path, kind, hashes, and the way to Bindings, Bundle and the lock diff in the Spec tree.
- **Links.** One link component takes 22 of the 36 call sites; 12 stay Spec tree links on purpose. A plain click opens the modal, and a modifier or middle click opens the page. A reload reopens the modal from one hash key, `doc` (Flow v2 Q4).
- **Flow's panel** shrinks to title, stage, work state, marks, the first paragraph, and Open document, New card and a group's Open; its Gaps control titles members from the read.
- **Unchanged.** The Spec tree, its own links included, stays the technical view (Flow v2 Q3).
- **RM-0091's** English plural fixes ship with this slice, with their tests.
