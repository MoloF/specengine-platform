# doc-modal (Phase 7, v5 step 3): one document modal everywhere, with the path as its breadcrumb

This is Flow v2's step 3d, together with RM-0091 `english-polish`. The owner's words (2026-11-10): the documents in Flow's right panel are unreadable, so he wants one modal that holds everything, a minimal panel, and technical fields out of the way. 8-12 pipeline hours.

- **The reads** serve each link end's `title` and `kind` in `show --links` and `get_node`.
- **The modal** works over any screen, with a page at `#/<p>/tree/<REF>/page`. It is RM-0093's work-item layout:
  - the breadcrumb from RM-0094's `path`;
  - the whole text at reading width;
  - links by title, cards and "Inside" (children by `parent:`);
  - the node's records behind "Records for the AI";
  - technical details folded: ID, path, kind, hashes, distance, and links to Bindings, Bundle and the lock diff.
- **Links.** One link component replaces about 34 call sites. A plain click opens the modal, and a modifier or middle click opens the page. A reload reopens the modal from one hash suffix (Flow v2 Q4).
- **Flow's panel** shrinks to title, stage, work state, the first paragraph, and Open, New card and Enter.
- **Unchanged.** The Spec tree stays the technical view (Flow v2 Q3).
- **RM-0091's** English plural fixes ship with this slice, with their tests.
