# english-polish: the UI's English grammar slips, fixed with their tests

Shipped 2026-11-11 in RM-0095 `doc-modal`'s slice (`docs/features/doc-modal.md`, D10, DM-17), which reworked the same strings. The sweep (RM-0090) had kept English byte-equal to the strings it replaced, slips included: a plural group whose `_one` repeated `_other` read wrong at 1 ("1 links not shown", "1 tokens", "1 holders").

Thirteen `_one` forms in `ui/src/i18n/en.ts` are fixed (six known, seven more found by a sweep of every plural group); `ui/src/i18n/plural.test.ts` reads each at 1 and 2, so restoring a slip turns it red. Russian keeps its forms. `docs/canon/ui-languages.md` "Known limits" dropped the line.
