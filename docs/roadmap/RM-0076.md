# flow-linear (Phase 7, step 12a): a flow read linear in `parent:` depth, a bundle fit that estimates each part once

Split from RM-0054 on 2026-11-03, the first of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices (the orchestrator's decision, awaiting the owner's review). `spec flow` and `spec trace` build each member's whole `parent:` chain to find its parent, and the depth fold climbs it again per call, so a read is quadratic in depth (`docs/canon/spec-flow.md` "Known limits"). `spec bundle` re-estimates the whole body for every candidate (`docs/canon/spec-cli-bundle.md` "Open"). One memoised document-to-parent map, with the ancestor walk stopping at an ancestor already walked, makes the flow linear. Running sums of characters and thousandths make exactly the decision the concatenation makes, rounding once. Every output stays byte for byte; no store change, no ADR. Spec: `docs/features/flow-linear.md`. 1-1.5 pipeline hours.
