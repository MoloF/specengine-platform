# flow-linear (Phase 7, step 12a): a flow read linear in `parent:` depth, a bundle fit that estimates each part once

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/flow-linear.md`, `docs/canon/spec-flow.md` "Code", `docs/canon/spec-cli-bundle.md` "Fitting"), the first of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices; the split is the orchestrator's, awaiting the owner's review. `spec flow` and `spec trace` take each file's parent document once per read and fold by depth once, so a `parent:` chain of 8 000 reads in at most 8x the time of 2 000, and the core test walks 20 000. `spec bundle` decides by running sums of characters and thousandths, rounded once (core `tokens::thousandths`, `tokens_of`), so each candidate is estimated once. Every output is byte for byte what it was; no store change, no ADR. Accepted on the first iteration; the second took a genre line and two nits.
