# canvas-clicks (Phase 7, v5 pool): Graph and Roadmap keep their clicks, and a lazy route survives a redeploy

Two follow-ups the UI review of RM-0098 `handoff-progress` named (`docs/features/handoff-progress.md` "Implementation"). UI only. Taken only if the pipeline trial or the owner asks for it.

- **Clicks on Graph and Roadmap.** Flow's fix (`docs/canon/spec-flow-layout.md` "Layout", Clicks) is Flow's alone. Graph and Roadmap keep xyflow 12.12.0's 1 px pane click distance, so a press that moves 2-3 px pans and loses the click, and their minimaps take the pointer over a box. The fix: the same press distance (`FLOW_CLICK_DISTANCE` 5) on both canvases, `pointer-events: none` on their minimaps, and the `:focus-visible` rule if either centres on focus. About 1-2 pipeline hours.
- **A lazy route after a redeploy.** `RoadmapView`, `GraphView` and the other lazy chunks (`docs/canon/ui-languages.md` "Size") carry content hashes. After a redeploy a page loaded before it asks for a chunk that is gone, and the route's Retry fails again until the page is reloaded. The fix: on `vite:preloadError`, reload the page once, guarded against a loop (a `sessionStorage` mark), so a viewer lands on the new build. About 1 pipeline hour.
