# canvas-clicks (Phase 7, v5): Graph and Roadmap keep their clicks, and a lazy route survives a redeploy

Two follow-ups the UI review of RM-0098 `handoff-progress` named (`docs/features/handoff-progress.md` "Implementation"). UI only. Taken into `v5` by the owner's word of 2026-11-12 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-12"): the UI lane's first slice, before RM-0099; its spec `docs/features/canvas-clicks.md`. 2-3 pipeline hours (its analysis of 2026-11-12).

- **Clicks on Graph and Roadmap.** Flow's fix (`docs/canon/spec-flow-layout.md` "Layout", Clicks) is Flow's alone. Graph and Roadmap keep xyflow 12.12.0's 1 px pane click distance, so a press that moves 2-3 px pans and loses the click, Graph's minimap takes the pointer over a box (Roadmap's Chain draws none), and Graph centres a box a mouse press focuses. The fix: one press distance (5 px) for the three canvases, `pointer-events: none` on Graph's minimap, and Flow's `:focus-visible` rule on Graph.
- **A lazy route after a redeploy.** `RoadmapView`, `GraphView` and the other lazy chunks (`docs/canon/ui-languages.md` "Size") carry content hashes. After a redeploy a page loaded before it asks for a chunk that is gone, and the route's Retry fails again until the page is reloaded. The fix: on `vite:preloadError`, reload the page once, guarded against a loop (a `sessionStorage` mark), so a viewer lands on the new build.
