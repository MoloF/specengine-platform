# hub-confirm (Phase 7, step 9b): one click confirms one staged choice, a commit in the hub's checkout, nothing pushed

A zone's lead or the owner, a login `[team]` entitles (RM-0071), confirms through the UI: one new route, hub only, takes a stage, never a choice, and applies it as a commit in the hub's own checkout, no fetch, merge or push. No always-yes consent and no `approve` call in the daemon. Issues `hub-confirm`, which narrows ADR-0035's "the only confirmation" and ADR-0038's "the UI only stages" on a hub alone, with no `supersedes:` key, and the `CLAUDE.md` rule. `crates/specengine-http/tests/door.rs` keeps its tests as written and gains `HUB_CONFIRM_CALLS`; a local daemon's router and fence do not change; ADR-0054's forms stay stage-only and local. No MCP tool or credential reaches a confirm route. Spec: `docs/features/hub-confirm.md`. 3-4 pipeline hours.

- **Who confirms.** The login door says who enters, `[team]` who confirms: a `lead` whose zones cover every target, or the `owner`. The record keeps the login; `name` is shown from here, never stored in its place, since a rename would break `by` and reviewer compares.
- **`Decided-by` and `Author`.** The two promises `hub-identity` could not keep without a confirm route: two identities give two `Decided-by` values, `<login> <email>` from `[team]`, and `Author` names a person, on a departing accept. Its AC-05 is taken here in full, M a default committer.
- **One click per item.** The route names one proposal and no route takes a set: a round stages, then each item takes its own click.
- **Where 9 ends.** At apply step 10, on the recorded branch of the hub's checkout. Step 10b, `hub-git`, begins at the first network verb, so it has its caller.
- **A stage replaced after the read.** The stage's compare runs on a one-second clock, so many logins can race a stage: last wins, each event names its `as` (`docs/canon/hub-team.md` "Stages"). So the confirm takes the staged value into its compare, never `updated_at` alone: that is what B5 of `docs/features/hub-confirm.md`, a stage replaced after the read is a 409, stands on.
- **The step-up.** The confirm routes' regex and the guarantee that keeps it are RM-0064's; the daemon cannot see a step-up, so it gives the proxy one byte form of each route.
