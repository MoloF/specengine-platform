# card-trace (Phase 7, step 6): targets, suspect marks, clearing, re-estimates, rollup

The trace from brief to result, needing no code. A node's cards are a join per read, so a card makes no commit. A card is suspect when a direct target's `norm_hash` changes, never its `span_hash` and never on an `--editorial` approve; direct targets only, to avoid floods. Clearing records a human outcome and a reason, in bulk by group, and the next meaning change restores the mark; a re-estimate is an event with old, new and reason; a correction card links by `corrects`. Rollup per node, computed per read, never stored: `no card`, `in work`, `suspect`, `departed`, `confirmed` (at least one card, all accepted or cancelled with at least one accepted, none suspect or departed). Acceptance asks "as briefed" or "departs"; a departure raises the discrepancy in the same transaction. Spec: `docs/features/card-trace.md`. 4-6 pipeline hours.
