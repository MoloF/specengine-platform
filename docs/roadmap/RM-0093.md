# design-foundation (Phase 7, v5 step 1): light and dark tokens, Inter, a type scale, primitives, the task as a work item

Shipped 2026-11-10 (`docs/features/design-foundation.md`; `docs/canon/ui-design.md`). UI only, no ADR. The owner found sizes and blocks inconsistent ("looks hand-made") and wants the look of a mature tracker. 12-17 pipeline hours: 7-10, plus 4-6 for the light theme and the font. First-load JS 806.03 kB of 845; the fonts 178 032 B, outside it.

- **Tokens.** Light and dark themes as semantic roles: surfaces, text, borders, an accent of SpecEngine's own, and the six lozenge appearances. Light is the default, dark follows the system, and a Theme select is remembered per viewer. A contrast matrix holds in both themes.
- **Type and space.** Inter is bundled under the OFL with Cyrillic, from committed `woff2` files, so no JS and no font host. The type scale is in rem (12/16, 14/20, 16/24; headings 24/28, 20/24, 16/20). Spacing runs 2-48 on an 8 px base, radii are 2/4/6/8/12, and controls are 32 px or 24 px compact.
- **Primitives** in `src/ui/`: Button, Lozenge, PropertyList, Breadcrumbs, IssueView, EmptyState, Disclosure. A status category comes from the column's position.
- **First use.** The card modal and page become the first work-item view: breadcrumbs, a main column, and a side column of at most 330 px with status and Move, the details, "More fields" and a folded Development. Board column heads take category lozenges and tiles take lozenge-sized badges. The header is one line.
- **Guards.** Tests fail on a raw font size, line height, spacing or radius outside the tokens; an exceptions list gives each a reason. The other screens migrate in RM-0099.
