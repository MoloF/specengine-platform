# handoff-progress (Phase 7, v5 step 6): a parent shows Ready to accept and a progress bar; Flow left to right again

Shipped 2026-11-11 (`docs/features/handoff-progress.md`; `docs/canon/card-progress.md`, `spec-flow-layout.md`), UI only, no ADR, no plugin change. It answers the owner's v5 Q3: a parent task shows "Ready to accept" and a progress bar, and a person accepts it; nothing closes a parent on its own (ADR-0074). It also took his four requests of 2026-11-11: step 6 alone, plus three fixes. First-load JS 815.11 kB of 845.

- **The read.** The task reads its subtasks, cancelled ones too, with one list read, and each cancelled one's reason with one card read. Nothing served changes.
- **The bar**, segmented: accepted, cancelled, then returned, in progress, to do, not in the flow, each with words and an icon.
- **The hint.** "Ready to accept" once every subtask is accepted or cancelled; it never gates, hides or refuses an act.
- **The lists.** "Relies on" names the open subtasks, never an item or an agent task; cancelled subtasks show their reasons.
- **Size first.** `RoadmapView` and `GraphView` left first load (840.79 kB before).
- **The owner's fixes.** Flow left to right again (Q14 closed by his look), about 2.5 columns in view at 1440 with both panes; a press on a Flow box moving up to 5 px is a click, the minimap and the stage dividers take no pointer, a press moves no box; task and document modals 1200 px with a 360 px side; arrows named from source to target.
- **Follow-up** in the pool: RM-0103 `canvas-clicks`, the same click fix for Graph and Roadmap, and a lazy route's reload after a redeploy.
- **Next**: the pipeline trial, a gate with no code (08 s2 Phase 7, "v5 scope"); the owner's stop point.
