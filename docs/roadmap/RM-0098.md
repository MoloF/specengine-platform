# handoff-progress (Phase 7, v5 step 6): a parent shows Ready to accept and a progress bar

The owner's answer of 2026-11-10: a parent task shows a "Ready to accept" hint and a progress bar, and a person accepts it. Nothing closes a parent on its own (ADR-0074 stands). UI only, from the rollup the card read already serves. 3-5 pipeline hours.

- **The bar.** It is segmented by RM-0093's status category, for example "3 accepted · 1 returned · 1 in progress", in label and icon, never colour alone.
- **The hint.** "Ready to accept" shows once every subtask is accepted or is cancelled with its reason shown. It never gates, hides or refuses an act.
- **The list.** Cancelled subtasks are listed with their reasons, and "Relies on" names what the task waits on, as words only.
- **The pipeline trial**, a gate with no code, follows this step (08 s2 Phase 7, "v5 scope").
