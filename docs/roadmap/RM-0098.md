# handoff-progress (Phase 7, v5 step 6): a parent shows Ready to accept and a progress bar; Flow left to right again

The spec is `docs/features/handoff-progress.md` (draft, 2026-11-11, 22 criteria). It is UI only and issues no ADR.

It answers the owner's answer of 2026-11-10 (Q3): a parent task shows "Ready to accept" and a progress bar, and a person accepts it. Nothing closes a parent on its own (ADR-0074 stands). It also takes his four requests of 2026-11-11: step 6 alone, plus three fixes. 9-14 pipeline hours, the UI lane alone.

- **The read.** The task reads its subtasks, cancelled ones too, with one list read. It reads each cancelled one's reason with one card read. Nothing served changes.
- **The bar.** It is segmented: accepted, cancelled, then returned, in progress, to do, not in the flow, each with words and an icon, never colour alone.
- **The hint.** "Ready to accept" shows once every subtask is accepted or cancelled. It never gates, hides or refuses an act.
- **The lists.** "Relies on" names the open subtasks, never an item or an agent task. Cancelled subtasks show their reasons.
- **Size first.** `RoadmapView`, then `GraphView`, leave first load (840.79 kB of 845) before the slice's own bytes.
- **The owner's fixes.**
  - Flow runs left to right again: Q14 is closed by his look.
  - A press on a Flow box that moves up to 5 px is a click. The minimap and the stage dividers take no pointer, and a press moves no box.
  - The task and document modals are 1200 px wide, with a 360 px side.
  - Arrows are named in reading order.
- **The pipeline trial**, a gate with no code, follows this step (08 s2 Phase 7, "v5 scope").
