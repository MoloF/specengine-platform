# comments (Phase 7, pool): talk under cards, nodes and proposals, as queue data

From the owner's request of 2026-11-21 ("comments and notes under tasks, like Jira") and his decisions of 2026-11-22 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-21/22"). It is slice 1 of three; the next are RM-0137 `work-references` and RM-0138 `reference-promotion`.

A comment is queue data, never truth (ADR-0087). It sits under a card, a node or a proposal, and is authored as a queue item is. Only its author edits it, each edit kept as a version. Its author or a `[team]` `owner` deletes it, and so can anyone at a terminal; a delete erases every version's text. Mentions get their own view. "Turn into…" makes a question, decision or discrepancy that names the comment. A card's context carries its newest comments outside the bundle, marked as data for agents.

Shipped 2026-11-22 as plugin 0.50.0 (MINOR), tag `v0.50.0`: `docs/features/comments.md` (CM1-CM21; CM19's soak and CM21, the owner's gate, open), canon `docs/canon/comments.md` and `comments-surfaces.md`. Queue schema 15 and dump format 8 mean the hub's upgrade takes a dump first, and every binary is reinstalled from the tag. Nothing blocks on it (ADR-0012).
