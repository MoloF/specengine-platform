# board-handoff (Phase 7, v4 step 3a): a board that holds still, a card modal and page, swimlanes, hand-off and return

The spec is `docs/features/board-handoff.md`, with ADR-0074 `card-handoff` (`docs/canon/card-flows.md` "The rule"). The board, like Jira's, has columns that never shift and swimlanes by assignee or parent. A plain click opens a card in a modal; a Cmd-click opens its page through a native link. The tabs from the workspace's card flows shipped with 1a. 9-12 pipeline hours.

- **Hand-off.** A hand-off is a child card in the receiving trade's flow, its reviewer the asking side, made in one act by `spec card new --reviewer`. The receiver takes it with a move.
- **Return.** A return is a move into the flow's one configured `return` column, with a reason, by `spec card move --note`. Only the child moves (the owner's Q1 of 2026-11-09). The parent shows a rollup of its children.
- **Acceptance.** Accepting the result stays the asking side's act (ADR-0056).
- **Reads.** `spec card list --parent`; MCP `create_card {reviewer}`, `move_card {note}`, `list_cards {parent}`; plugin 0.31.0.

Tasks inside cards moved to 3b, RM-0092 `card-tasks`.
