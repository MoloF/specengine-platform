# work-references (Phase 7, pool): photos, sound, models and links attached to work, reaching its agent

From the owner's request of 2026-11-21: a writer attaches a photo of a location, a model or a sound to a task, and it is used later. Under his decision of 2026-11-22, uploads live on the hub with no off-host copy, a loss risk he accepts (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-21/22"). Slice 2 of three, after RM-0136; next RM-0138.

A reference, a file or a link, is queue data under a card, a node or a proposal, authored and deleted as a comment (ADR-0088). A file's bytes live in a BLAKE3-named store in the data directory, a hub's own volume `specengine-blobs`, never in a repository, a dump or an off-host copy. One binary route streams an upload outside the turn; the type is its sniffed bytes; 256 MiB a file, 10 GiB a project, 5 GiB kept free; files served sandboxed. Images reach agents as MCP image content up to 1 568 px and 1 MiB, else a browser-made copy; audio, video, models and PDF need "what matters here".

Shipped 2026-11-23 as plugin 0.51.0 (MINOR), tag `v0.51.0`: `docs/features/work-references.md` (RF1-RF26; the RF23 soak and the owner's gates RF25, RF26 open), canon `docs/canon/work-references.md` and `work-references-surfaces.md`. Queue schema 16 and dump format 9: the hub dumps first, every binary comes from the tag, and `up -d` adds the volume. Nothing blocks on it (ADR-0012).
