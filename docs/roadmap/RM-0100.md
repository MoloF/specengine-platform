# department-split (Phase 7, v5 step 6c): a task and a subtask per department, in one act

Shipped 2026-11-11 (`docs/features/department-split.md`; `docs/canon/card-split.md`), with ADR-0078 `card-split`, which supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0076. Re-scoped on 2026-11-11 from `journeys` (optional, a soft `after`) by the owner's vision (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): a writer describes the game, and it is split into subtasks per department. Plugin 0.37.0; first-load JS 818.68 kB of 845.

- **One act**: a parent task on a document, or a stored task, and one subtask per chosen discipline, each with the split's reviewer, placed by the new-card rule; one transaction, all or none.
- **Templates**: `[[card.split]] kind = ... disciplines = [...]` under `[card]` pre-selects disciplines and picks each subtask's targets, the nodes of its kinds, else the parent's; it never refuses.
- **Interfaces**: `spec card split` and its `--dry-run`, MCP `split_card` (30 tools on stdio, 18 on a hub), `POST .../cards/split`, a hub's seventh plain write; one lazy dialog from the document, Flow's panel and a task.
- **The preset** gains a `sound` flow and department and commented split rows; `card-press` carries two live rows.
- **Open**: the owner's Q-A to Q-C; subtasks carry the parent's title (Q-C).
- **Next**: 6d `task-mandate` (RM-0104), then the owner's stop point.
