# delivery-analytics (Phase 7, v5): what was delivered, against its estimate, and why it took longer

From the owner's vision of 2026-11-11 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): a roadmap of delivered features, where we are, how much is left, what took longer and why. Per task: created, taken out of its first column, accepted, against its estimate and due date; lateness with its reasons (return notes, questions, cancelled subtasks); rolled up by release and by department. Derived per read from card rows and events. It absorbs RM-0087's timeline view, with release markers; RM-0087 keeps the states. 8-12 pipeline hours (its analysis of 2026-11-12).

Taken into `v5` by the owner's word of 2026-11-12 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-12"), in two parts: (a) the read and its tables, a Delivery tab, no MCP tool, the sixth core slice; (b) the timeline, the last.

Part a, `delivery-analytics` (`docs/features/delivery-analytics.md`), shipped 2026-11-12: `spec delivery [--as-of D]`, the daemon's `GET .../delivery` and the Roadmap's lazy Delivery tab count, per release and per department, tasks delivered of tasks, open, overdue, delivered late and cancelled, with lead days, then list each late task with its returns, re-estimates, cancelled subtasks and items. A card's release is the first plan item it or an ancestor targets; computed per read, never stored; no MCP tool, plugin version or schema step. The rules are canon: `docs/canon/spec-delivery.md`, `docs/canon/spec-roadmap-screen.md` "Delivery". Part b, `delivery-timeline` (`docs/features/delivery-timeline.md`), shipped 2026-11-13, closing the item: `spec delivery --timeline` and `GET .../delivery?as_of=D&timeline=true` add every task not cancelled on one axis (created, taken, accepted, due, days late) with a marker per group, its first take and last accept, from the same read; the Delivery tab's lazy Timeline view and its table twin; no MCP tool, plugin version or schema step (`docs/canon/spec-delivery-timeline.md`).
