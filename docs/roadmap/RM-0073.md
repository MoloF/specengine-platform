# spec-ids (Phase 7): spec IDs that a hub and a terminal never mint twice

Record IDs (`docs/canon/decision-record.md` "ID": the greater of the root's index and the queue's issued `record_id`) and created node IDs (`docs/canon/proposal-kinds.md` "Reservation") are minted per queue. So a hub and a terminal, or two terminals, can mint the same one. Once `hub-git` (RM-0051) pushes and `hub-mcp` (RM-0053) lets a hub raise creates and decisions, an add/add conflict or `id-taken` after a merge shows such a collision, and a person renumbers. `apply-outputs` (RM-0072) states this as a known limit; its cure moved here from RM-0053. This item picks the cure and says what an older binary does with it, never refusing an apply (ADR-0012). The candidates: a hub's own range for record numbers, as `PR-` has its floor; a reservation read from the merged tree before a mint; a renumbering proposal raised on the conflict. Unestimated.

Placed on 2026-11-04 right after step 12 (RM-0054) and before 11b-2 `hub-ops` (RM-0074), in `v3`, the hosted hub's release: the order is the owner's instruction (08 s2 Phase 7, "Release scope").
