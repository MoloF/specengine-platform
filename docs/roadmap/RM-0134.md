# mixed-load-memory (pool): about 200 MB held between calls under writers and readers together

Found 2026-11-20 by write-memory's WM8 (RM-0132), outside its fix: the live-heap judge passes it. It is a must under ADR-0086, and the next slice.

**Evidence**, from both M runs (8 `ask_question` writers beside 10 readers of `get_node`, `search` and `GET …/inbox`, 300 s; `docs/canon/soak-runs.md` "Known limits"):
- RSS stays about 100 MB until about 200 s, then climbs about 110 MiB/min to about 300 MB by 299 s.
- The footprint goes 36 → 230 MB, +194 MiB/min at the quiet moments in the last third.
- The live heap reads 1 750 KiB at every reading.

That is about 200 MB of dirty memory held between calls, which malloc does not count as allocated. W alone (3 135 stored) and R alone stay flat. One heap-mode M run, slower with 1 597 stored, did not reproduce it; its `vmmap` showed 98 % fragmentation and "Malloc Small (empty)" 79 MB resident.

**Hypothesis, unconfirmed:** `GET …/inbox` lists every pending item (`crates/specengine-http/src/app.rs`, `all: false`, no limit). Past about 1 800 pending items, ten concurrent inbox reads have a working set that libmalloc keeps.

**The task asks for:**
- M without the inbox readers, and R over a pre-filled queue of about 2 000 pending items, two runs each;
- the cause, named with its evidence, and a targeted fix accepted by a clean re-run of M;
- a judge that also sees memory malloc does not count (the footprint at the quiet moments, beside the live heap), with a written budget.

Nothing blocks on it (ADR-0012).
