# scale-eval (Phase 7, step 12b): a seeded synthetic corpus and today's read cost at 1 000, 10 000 and 100 000 nodes

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/scale-eval.md`, `docs/canon/scale-eval.md`), the second of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices. The split and Q2, Q8 are the orchestrator's and await the owner's review.

`specengine-eval scale` does four things:
- It generates a seeded, domain-free corpus: about five nodes per file, target in-degree capped at 16, search hits bounded, and four hubs measured apart.
- It times the warm `get_node` (with and without links), `search` and `get_context_bundle` through the CLI library, as MCP calls them: release, best of three.
- It replays each step of those reads alone.
- It computes W_after by `w`'s formula.

There is no product change and no ADR. The work was accepted on the first iteration.

Today's baseline, at 100 000 nodes:
- The reads take 705, 1 638, 424 and 1 613 ms, about 11x their 10 000-node figures, so they are linear.
- Reparse (798 ms), hash (393) and decode (249) dominate.
- W_after's median is already flat, at 2 210, 2 182 and 2 239 B for 1 000, 10 000 and 100 000.
