# scale-eval (Phase 7, step 12b): a seeded synthetic corpus and today's read cost at 1 000, 10 000 and 100 000 nodes

Split from RM-0054 on 2026-11-03 (the orchestrator's decision, awaiting the owner's review). It measures before 12c-12e change anything, so each is judged against today. 1.5-2 pipeline hours.

- **The command (Q8, the orchestrator's, awaiting the owner's review).** A new `specengine-eval scale`: a seeded generator, timings, and W_after by `w`'s formula, under `--timeout`.
- **The corpus (Q2, the orchestrator's, awaiting the owner's review).** About five nodes per file, up to about 20 000 files; target degree and query hits bounded. Highly linked nodes are reported apart, with no bar. No domain word and no pilot's structure.
- **The baseline.** Release build, best of three, at 1 000, 10 000 and 100 000 nodes: each read's time and its split per step (the walk and hashing, decoding the index, re-reading and re-parsing, resolving links, fitting). RM-0054's bars are measured by this command.
- **Cost.** Heavy runs are `#[ignore]`d; release runs grow `target.noindex`, cleaned at the slice's end.
