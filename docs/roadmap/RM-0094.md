# flow-scenario (Phase 7, v5 step 2): Flow draws the scenario, and its left pane is a path

Spec `docs/features/flow-scenario.md`, with no ADR. This slice joins Flow v2's step 3c with the owner's path request of 2026-11-10. Today Flow draws records and ID-less documents as a wall of tiny boxes, and its places pane shows a scope rather than a path. It takes 11-17 pipeline hours. The Rust lane starts at once; the UI lane waits for RM-0093 and works against the mock.

- **In the flow.** Every flow read serves a domain-free `in_flow` flag and the list of link `types`. An opt-in cut (`--in-flow`, `in_flow`) runs after the fold, keeps the ROOT, and counts in `not_in_flow` the documents it dropped. Default reads stay byte-equal.
- **The canvas** draws only what the cut keeps, as readable boxes. Flow gets its own fit floor of 0.92 for 14 px titles, and Graph keeps 0.6. The walk runs top to bottom with stage dividers (Q14). Off-walk edges show only for the selected box, with no `xN` badge on the canvas. A Gaps control sits in the canvas bar. "Records for the AI (N)" opens a list read on demand. Titles replace IDs, which fold under Technical details. The chevron in the places pane is fixed.
- **The path.** `show`, `get_node` and the nodes read serve `ancestors`: the path as `parent:` ancestors, top first, each as `{id, kind, title, path}`. `path` is not the key's name, because a node's `path` is already its file.
  - When a node has more than one holder, the first by `(path, ord)` is used. A dangling or `project:` parent ends the chain, and a cycle is listed once.
  - The list comes from the subset read's parent rows.
  - The places pane becomes the path, a labelled list of titles with flow-hash links and `aria-current`, with the current level's groups below it. A cold deep mount makes no extra read.
  - `flow.upStart` and the "Up from held reads" limit are removed.
- **Criteria.** FS-1 to FS-24 merge the analysts' FS-1 to FS-14 and FL-1 to FL-15. The new domain-free fixture `flow-aside` holds 10 steps, 4 stages, 14 parts, 142 records and 8 ID-less notes, and has a 40x variant built by the tests. The looks are taken at 1440x900, 1920x1080 and 1100x800 in both languages. The plugin goes to 0.33.0.
