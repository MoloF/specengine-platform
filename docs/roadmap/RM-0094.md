# flow-scenario (Phase 7, v5 step 2): Flow draws the scenario, and its left pane is a path

This is Flow v2's step 3c, together with the owner's path request of 2026-11-10. Flow now draws records and ID-less documents as a wall of tiny boxes, and its places pane shows a scope rather than a path. 11-17 pipeline hours.

- **In the flow.** Every flow read serves a domain-free `in_flow` flag, plus an opt-in cut after the fold (like `--stage`) that also counts what it removed. Default reads stay whole for the CLI and MCP.
- **The canvas** draws only flagged nodes, as readable boxes: Flow's own fit floor is at least 0.92, while Graph keeps 0.6. The walk runs top to bottom with stage dividers (Q14). Off-walk edges show only for the selected box, with no `xN` badge. A Gaps control sits in the canvas bar, and "Records for the AI · N" is a list read on demand. Titles replace IDs. The places-pane chevron is fixed.
- **The path.** `show`, `get_node` and the nodes read serve `path`: the `parent:` ancestors, top first, as `{id, title, kind}`. When a node has more than one holder, the first by `(path, ord)` is used. A dangling or project end stops the path, and a cycle is listed once. The places pane becomes the path, a labelled nav of titles with flow-hash links and `aria-current`, and lists the current level's groups below it. A cold deep mount makes no extra reads. `flow.upStart` and the "Up from held reads" limit are removed.
- **Criteria.** FL-1 to FL-15, plus Flow v2's FS-1 to FS-14. A domain-free fixture holds 10 steps, 4 stages, 14 parts, 142 records and 8 ID-less notes. The looks are taken at 1440x900, 1920x1080 and 1100x800 in both languages.
