# flow-scenario (Phase 7, v5 step 2): Flow draws the scenario, and its left pane is a path

Shipped 2026-11-10 (`docs/features/flow-scenario.md`; `docs/canon/spec-flow-cuts.md`, `spec-flow-bar.md`, `spec-flow-screen.md`). No ADR. Flow v2 step 3c joined with the owner's path request of 2026-11-10: Flow drew records and ID-less documents as a wall of tiny boxes, and its places pane showed a scope, not a path. Plugin 0.33.0; first-load JS 815.13 kB of 845.

- **In the flow.** Every flow read serves a domain-free `in_flow`, folded like `start`, and the model's link `types`. An opt-in cut (`--in-flow`, `in_flow`) runs after the fold and the stage, keeps the ROOT, and counts in `not_in_flow` the documents it dropped. Default reads stay byte-equal.
- **The canvas** draws what the cut keeps, top to bottom, one row per distance, with stage dividers and the off-walk band below; boxes 160 px, titles at 14 px, a fit floor of 0.92 (Graph keeps 0.6). Off-walk edges show only while an end is selected; no count on the canvas. A Gaps control sits in the bar; "Records for the AI (N)" opens a list read on demand. Titles replace IDs, which fold under Technical details.
- **The path.** `show`, `get_node` and the nodes read serve `ancestors`, the `parent:` chain top first, `{id, kind, title, path}`, from the subset read's `PARENT` rows. The places pane is that path; Up is gone.
