# task-mandate (Phase 7, v5 step 6d): a subtask is binding, or its department decides

Shipped 2026-11-12 (`docs/features/task-mandate.md`; `docs/canon/card-mandate.md`), with ADR-0079 `card-mandate`, which supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0077. From the owner's vision of 2026-11-11 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): no shifting of responsibility; a task is binding, or the department decides. Plugin 0.38.0; first-load JS 823.45 kB of 845.

- **The field**: `mandate` on the card, `binding`, `open` or null, named per subtask by the asking side, required in the Hand off and Split dialogs; `--mandate` needs a reviewer, who alone changes it.
- **Binding**: Return… and Ask a question… lead the acts, Return's question opens; Record a decision… stays (ADR-0012), noted binding, and the parent marks it a departure.
- **Department decides**: Record a decision… leads, its dialog requires a why (the store keeps it optional); the parent's "Decisions from subtasks", one read of its children's items, with Ask about it….
- **Nothing reads it**: no move, accept, rollup, stage, approve, confirm or gate.
- **Store**: queue schema 12, `CARD_COLUMNS` 26; backup format 6, importing 5, 4 and 3 with null.
- **Open**: the owner's Q-A to Q-C; the hub's upgrade, on his word.
- **Next**: the owner's stop point; the pipeline trial after a hub upgrade to 0.38.
