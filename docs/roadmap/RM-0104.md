# task-mandate (Phase 7, v5 step 6d): a subtask is binding, or its department decides

From the owner's vision of 2026-11-11 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): no shifting of responsibility; a task is binding, or the department decides. His draft case: "a new female character", whose voice the sound designer picks. An ADR `card-mandate`, issued with its spec, supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0077. 7-11 pipeline hours, plugin 0.38.0; after RM-0100, in its own spec.

- **The field**: `mandate` on the card row, `binding`, `open` or null. The asking side names it at creation, required in the Split and Hand off dialogs; `--mandate` needs `--reviewer`. Only the reviewer changes it, by `edit --mandate`, one `card.edited`.
- **Binding**: a departure goes up as a Return with a question, shown first; Record a decision stays (ADR-0012), noted "the task is binding".
- **Department decides**: Record a decision leads, and its dialog requires a why (a form rule; the store keeps `why` optional). The writer sees the decisions through `card_id`, the Inbox's task link and a new parent part, "Decisions from subtasks", one read of the subtasks' items.
- **Nothing reads it**: no move, accept, `allowed`, rollup, Ready to accept, stage, approve, confirm or gate.
- **Store**: queue schema 12, `mandate TEXT`, `CARD_COLUMNS` 26; backup format 6, importing 5, 4 and 3 with null; list rows, `card.created` and `card.edited` carry it.
- **Words**: stored `binding` and `open`; on screen "Binding" and "Department decides", never "Approved" or "Open", which are status words.
- **The hub** upgrades once, after this slice and RM-0100: a dump on `v0.36.0` first, copied off the host, then the new tag, then the config keys.
