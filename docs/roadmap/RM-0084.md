# writer-studio (Phase 7, v4 step 2b): Flow in three panes

The owner's layout for Flow, rebuilt in place on the `flow` route. 8-12 pipeline hours. If the slice has to be cut, 2c takes the List rework and gap-to-card.

- **Left pane.** The stages in walk order, with the scope's groups and flows under them. Every item is a link, and the Timeline folds into this pane.
- **The URL.** The hash holds the scope, the stage and the selection, so a reload and Back land in the same place. A scope change pushes history; a selection replaces it. Old `#/<p>/flow/<name>` links still select.
- **The centre.** A fixed canvas fills the viewport below the shell, and the wheel pans it. Above 64rem the page never scrolls. Selecting re-centres nothing.
- **Right pane.** Always mounted, at a fixed width, scrolling inside. With nothing selected it shows the scope: its about text, its gap rows and its actions.
- **Header and legend.** The header is one line. The legend is a one-line bar at the bottom edge that expands over the canvas.
- **On the canvas.** An edge click selects the edge. The Stage filter narrows through RM-0083's read. The List becomes a clickable outline and stays the canvas's text twin.
- **A gap handed to the AI.** "Hand to your AI" on a gap item is the New card form, preset with the item's members and its cause. It makes no `T-` task: Q9's working answer, an owner stop.
- **The mocks.** They drop their `depends_on` flow edges and serve `stage`.

The studio trial follows it, a gate with no code (08 s2 Phase 7, "v4 scope").
