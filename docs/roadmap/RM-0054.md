# read-trust (Phase 7, step 12e): read freshness, a hub's trusted index and the bars at 100 000 nodes

Re-scoped 2026-11-03 to the last of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices, after RM-0076 to RM-0079 (12a-12d, shipped). The orchestrator split the step while the owner was away; the split and Q1-Q6 below await the owner's review. Today every read walks the tree, `lstat`s each path component and hashes each file before it answers (`refresh` -> `update`), and opens a write transaction even when nothing changed. Since RM-0079 that freshness step is what `show`, `--links` and `bundle` cost, so the stat key and the trusted mode are what remain. This slice issues the ADR "read freshness" (Q6) as a working answer, with its canon. 2-3 pipeline hours.

- **Local (Q4, Q5).** The stat key is the full tuple: size, mtime ns, ctime ns, inode, device. The racy margin is 2 s, with no opt-out; `spec index --full` repairs. The code index stays on "never mtime".
- **Hub (Q3).** Writers update the index inside the turn: `update_paths` after an apply or a merge. The first read after start walks, and a dirty mark kept per process forces a walk (T4). Printed files are checked (T5, RM-0079). A stat reconcile runs inside the turn every 5 minutes. The operator's guide names `spec index`. No file watcher. A mode flag passes through http and mcp, and the hub's MCP texts that promise a refresh change (`crates/specengine-mcp/src/hub.rs:262`, `:339`).
- **Invariants.** T1: a trusted read equals the walking read byte for byte. T2: warnings are replayed. T3 (one index snapshot per read) and T5 hold since RM-0079 (`docs/canon/spec-read-subset.md`); a subset read cannot see an edit made after the freshness step outside its subset, and that window is this slice's.
- **Bars (Q1, Q2), measured by RM-0077's `specengine-eval scale`.** Warm `get_node`, `search` and `get_context_bundle` stay under 1 s at 100 000 nodes, their cost growing under 2x from 10 000. They measure the read's own work: the trusted path on a hub, a local read after its freshness step. The local stat walk reads no file when warm, and its time is reported. W_after's median at 10 000 stays within 10 % of 1 000. A synthetic corpus proves load, not usefulness: until this ships, keep a hub's first real project small.
- **Baseline (RM-0077, 2026-11-04, release).** Reads grow linearly, about 11x from 10 000; at 100 000, `get_node` 705 ms, `search` 424, `get_context_bundle` 1 613. Reparse (798 ms), hash (393) and decode (249) dominate; resolving links is 128. W_after's median already holds: 2 210, 2 182, 2 239 B. After RM-0079 (base `2220d05`, one session): at 100 000, `get_node` 372 ms, `get_node_links` 373, `search` 380, `get_context_bundle` 376, `update_us` 376 (hashing 349), the hub's links 398 and bundle 494; at 10 000, links 32.5 and bundle 32.7 (`docs/canon/scale-eval.md` "Baseline").
- **Fan-out (RM-0078).** A file citing every document, such as a generated `index*.md`, depends on every `path:` key, so any edit re-resolves it with its fan-out: about 0.25-0.4 s per update at 100 000 nodes, a reviewer's estimate. Not yet measured: measure `update_us` with a generated index before this slice sets bars (`docs/canon/spec-index-edges.md` "Known limits").
- **Nit from RM-0079.** Store `src/edges.rs:95`: the `PARENTED` comment says "RM-0079's reads", stale since 12d shipped without that lookup. Fix the comment or drop the lookup.
- **Risks.** A stale trusted index: an operator's merge on the host, a failed update after an apply, a fast-forward killed at the deadline (`docs/canon/hub-git.md` "Known limits"). A mixed state within one read (T3). Probing n paths in a directory of m entries cost n×m until RM-0078 batched the probes (`Source::probe_paths`).
