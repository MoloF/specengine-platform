# read-trust (Phase 7, step 12e): read freshness, a hub's trusted index and the bars at 100 000 nodes

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/read-trust.md`, `docs/canon/spec-read-freshness.md`, ADR-0069 `read-freshness`), the last of step 12 `scale-reads`' five slices: step 12 is closed. The split, Q1-Q6, D1-D16, the ADR and the decisions taken during implementation are the orchestrator's and await the owner's review.

- A read's freshness step reads a file only when its stat key (size, mtime and ctime in ns, inode, device) moved or was taken within 2 s of its last change; a no-op update opens no write transaction; `INDEX_FORMAT` 17 keeps the keys and the last walk's notes outside the dump; `spec index --full` repairs; the code index stays on content.
- On a hub alone, only reads trust: they answer from the index its confirms and merges update in the turn, replaying the last walk's warnings, and walk after start, after a doubt and every 5 minutes. Writes, authorizations, trace data and unclassified routes or tools walk. The operator's guide names `spec index` after a hand change on the host.
- Fan-out: one edited leaf's `update_paths` at 100 000 nodes took 7.0 ms, 409 ms beside a file citing every document, 519 ms of that file at the base (10.2, 422 and 536 ms at the head); under 1 s, so no plan item.
- The bars, all met: at 100 000 nodes trusted `get_node` 0.55 ms, links 0.85, `search` 6.5, bundle 2.4, against 372-380 at 12d's head; walking reads 43-50 ms, the warm update 41.9 ms reading no file; growth 1.00 from 10 000 (the 10 ms floor); W_after within 1.3 %; trusted answers equal to walking ones.
- Review gave ACCEPT on iterations 1 (minors), 2 and 3; all 25 named mutations killed, three only after new tests closed gaps.

Limits awaiting the owner: on a hub, a hand edit on the host is unseen by trusting reads until a reconcile (300 s) or `spec index`; doubt does not cross processes; a driver whose ctime does not move, or a network client's attribute cache, can hide a same-size edit until `--full`.

Follow-up, outside 12e: the probes build's `ac17_the_probes_build_sends_instructions_alone` (`crates/specengine-mcp/tests/mcp_create.rs:469-470`) asserts both `sent.len() == 2048` and `2048 - sent.len() == 8`, which no length satisfies. It predates 12e, and only the probes build runs it.
