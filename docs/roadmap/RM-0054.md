# scale-reads (Phase 7, step 12): an edge table, a trusted index, reads at 100 000 nodes

Today every read re-reads and re-hashes every file, and `spec flow` is quadratic in `parent:` depth (a chain of 1 000, 2 000, 4 000 took 0.58, 2.39, 9.79 s in debug). This adds a resolved-edge table, incremental, under the store's "equals a rebuild" property; the hub trusts its index, updated by `update_paths` after an apply or merge, and local mode takes a stat shortcut; the bundle keeps running counts. On synthetic corpora, warm `get_node`, `search` and `get_context_bundle` stay under 1 s at 100 000 nodes with cost growing under 2x from 10 000, and W_after's median at 10 000 stays within 10 % of 1 000. A synthetic corpus proves load, not usefulness. Until this ships, keep a hub's first real project small. 6-10 pipeline hours.
