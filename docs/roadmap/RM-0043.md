# flow-read (Phase 7, step 2): `spec flow`, `get_flow`, lifted edges and gap rows

`spec flow [ROOT] [--depth N]`, MCP `get_flow` and the daemon's route: the walk over `follows`, typed edges, lifted edges with counts when a group collapses, and gap rows in the form of ADR-0047 (computed per read, stopping nothing). An endless flow needs no new word: an end step may carry a `repeat` edge to itself, and a loop that can reach an end is no trap. Spec: `docs/features/flow-read.md`. 3-5 pipeline hours.
