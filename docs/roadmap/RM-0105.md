# upstream-signal (Phase 7, pool): a subtask shows that its parent changed above it

From the owner's vision of 2026-11-11 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): a correction is made at the top first, and the work below must see it. A subtask is marked when, after it was created, its parent's goal, criteria or targets changed, or a decision on one of its targets was applied. Derived per read from the parent's events and the targets' applied records, never stored; it never moves, refuses or gates a card (ADR-0012, ADR-0074). Taken after the pipeline trial. 3-5 pipeline hours.
