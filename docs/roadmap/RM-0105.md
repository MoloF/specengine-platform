# upstream-signal (Phase 7, v5): a subtask shows that its parent changed above it

Shipped 2026-11-12 (`docs/features/upstream-signal.md`; `docs/canon/card-upstream.md`), no ADR. From the owner's vision of 2026-11-11 (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's vision, 2026-11-11"): a correction is made at the top first, and the work below must see it. An open subtask carries `upstream`: its parent's goal, criteria or targets edited, a question, discrepancy or decision applied on their nodes, or the parent reading suspect or correcting, since it was made, by `seq`; its own edit of goal, criteria or targets answers what came before. Per read from the queue's events and the index, never stored; it moves, refuses and gates nothing (ADR-0012, ADR-0074). `spec card show --marks`, `get_card` list the changes, `card list --marks` and `list_cards` flag the row, the Board's tile says "Changed above" and the work item lists them.
