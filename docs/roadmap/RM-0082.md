# ui-languages (Phase 7, v4 step 1b): the chrome in English and Russian

The owner's decision 2 of 2026-11-08: the UI in Russian and English, switchable. Issues the ADR `ui-languages`. It supersedes ADR-0014's English-only interface, amends the owner's decision 10 of 2026-10-30 ("English chrome"), and narrows ADR-0024 to the catalog values in `ui/`. Waits for the owner on Q1 (Russian catalog values in the repository) and Q2 (no i18n library). 3-5 pipeline hours.

- A typed English catalog is the source of the keys, and the Russian catalog is typed against it. Plurals and dates use `Intl.PluralRules` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat`. `<html lang>` follows the choice.
- The switcher keeps the choice per viewer in `localStorage`. A first visit follows the browser's language if it is Russian, else English.
- The shell, the nav, the palette, Home and the labels' meanings are converted here. Each other screen is converted by the slice that next touches it, with a listed sweep before the studio trial.
- Single-key hotkeys match the physical key (`event.code`), so they work under a Russian layout.
- Only the chrome is translated (Q3). Spec content shows as written. The daemon's, the CLI's and MCP's texts, refusals, gap causes and notes stay English, verbatim.
