# ui-languages (Phase 7, v4 step 1b): the chrome in English and Russian

The owner's decision 2 of 2026-11-08: the UI in Russian and English, switchable. His answers of 2026-11-09: Q1, Russian text in the repository only in the UI's translation catalog, with keys, comments, tests and docs in English; Q2, "use any good and reliable library", for which the orchestrator picked i18next with react-i18next. Issues ADR-0072 `ui-languages`, accepted with the spec (`docs/features/ui-languages.md`). It replaces the English-only interface, narrows ADR-0024 to the one file `ui/src/i18n/ru.ts`, and amends the owner's decision 10 of 2026-10-30 ("English chrome"). It goes before 2b (RM-0084), whose strings join its catalog. 3-5 pipeline hours.

- **The catalog.** One catalog in `ui/src/i18n/`. English is the source of the keys, and Russian is typed against it. The ui-developer writes the Russian and the owner reviews it: an open item, not a blocker. Plurals go through i18next over `Intl.PluralRules`, dates through `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, and `<html lang>` follows the choice.
- **The switcher.** A Language select sits in the header, and the choice is kept per viewer in `localStorage`. A first visit follows a Russian browser, else English.
- **Converted here.** The shell, the nav, the palette, Home, the shared `src/ui/` parts, the six `labels.ts` and 1a's strings. Flow converts with 2b, and the sweep (RM-0090) converts the rest before the studio trial.
- **Hotkeys.** A letter hotkey matches the letter a Latin layout gives (`letterOf`), so it works under a Russian layout.
- **Only the chrome is translated** (Q3, a working answer). Spec content shows as written. The daemon's, the CLI's and MCP's texts stay English, verbatim.
