# ui-languages (Phase 7, v4 step 1b): the chrome in English and Russian

Shipped 2026-11-09 (`docs/features/ui-languages.md`; `docs/canon/ui-languages.md`), under ADR-0072 `ui-languages`, which supersedes the English-only interface (ADR-0014), narrows ADR-0024 to the one file `ui/src/i18n/ru.ts` and amends the owner's decision 10 of 2026-10-30. The owner's decision 2 of 2026-11-08 and his Q1 and Q2 of 2026-11-09; the orchestrator picked i18next 26.5.11 with react-i18next 17.1.9. Open: the owner's review of the Russian and of the departures its spec's "Implementation" records.

- **The catalog.** One catalog in `ui/src/i18n/`. English is the source of the keys, and Russian is typed against it. The ui-developer wrote the Russian; the owner reviews it, not a blocker. Plurals go through i18next over `Intl.PluralRules`, dates through `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, and `<html lang>` follows the choice.
- **The switcher.** A Language select sits in the header, and the choice is kept per viewer in `localStorage`. A first visit follows a Russian browser, else English.
- **Converted.** The shell, the nav, the palette, Home, the shared `src/ui/` parts, the six `labels.ts` and 1a's strings. Flow converts with 2b, and the sweep (RM-0090) converts the rest before the studio trial.
- **Hotkeys.** A letter hotkey matches the letter a Latin layout gives (`letterOf`): under a Russian layout the physical key acts, and a Latin layout keeps its own letters.
- **Only the chrome is translated** (Q3, a working answer). Spec content shows as written. The daemon's, the CLI's and MCP's texts stay English, verbatim.
