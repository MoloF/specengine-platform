# plain-questions (Phase 7, v5 step 10): a question's gist and options, collisions as pairs

Today's questions are walls of IDs, file paths and code names. `ask_question` gains three fields, mirroring a discrepancy's options and recommendation:
- a one-line gist;
- why it matters;
- 2-3 options, each with its consequences.

The AI's recommendation is labelled as the AI's. The Questions screen and a task's questions (RM-0097) show the gist and the options first. The MCP descriptions ask for plain words. The engine structures and folds, and never rewrites an agent's text, since there is no LLM inside.

Collisions show as pairs, "what conflicts with what", side by side under their titles, from a served `pair` for a two-target `contradicts`. They are built on RM-0060 `collision-pass`.

It ships in two halves (08 s2 Phase 7, "Owner's word, 2026-11-12"): the question half, queue schema 13 and backup format 7, the eighth core slice, after RM-0099's sweep and waiting on neither RM-0059 nor RM-0060; the pair half after RM-0060, the tenth. 9-13 pipeline hours (its analysis of 2026-11-12): 6-9 and 3-4.

Re-scoped 2026-11-10 (08 s2 Phase 7, "v5 scope"). The general rule of titles over IDs, with IDs, paths and spans folded under technical details, moves to RM-0095 for documents and RM-0099 for the Questions screen. What is left here is the question's own structure and the pairs.
