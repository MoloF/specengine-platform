# flow-scope (Phase 7, v4 step 2a): Flow back to three link types, `--stage` that narrows, and the flow reads measured

Plan items stand as top boxes on Flow, and the Stage filter only dims. Two changes fix that.

- `FLOW_LINK_TYPES` goes back to ADR-0055's three (Q4). `depends_on` orders a plan, never a flow. The canon agreed with itself on this only until `flow-read`; it does again now, with no new ADR.
- `spec flow --stage S` (`-` for none), `get_flow {stage}` and `GET .../flow?stage=` keep, of a read's shown nodes, those of one stage, and the edges between them. Gap rows and every kept node stay as the read without the flag has them.

`specengine-eval scale` times five reads: the Top read, a group's read, the widest stage read, `spec trace --summary` and the tree. The synthetic corpus gains a flow shape so these reads have something to read. Runs parallel with 1a and 1b, and needs nothing from the owner. Spec: `docs/features/flow-scope.md`. 3-4 pipeline hours by the analysis, plus 0.5-1 for the corpus's flow shape.
