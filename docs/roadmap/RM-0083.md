# flow-scope (Phase 7, v4 step 2a): Flow back to three link types, `--stage` that narrows, and the flow reads measured

Shipped 2026-11-09 (`docs/features/flow-scope.md`; `docs/canon/spec-flow.md`, `docs/canon/scale-eval.md`), with no new ADR; Q4 is the orchestrator's working answer, awaiting the owner's review.

- **Three link types.** `FLOW_LINK_TYPES` is ADR-0055's three again: `depends_on` orders a plan, never a flow, so plan items no longer stand as top boxes on Flow.
- **`--stage`.** `spec flow --stage S` (`-` for none), `get_flow {stage}` and `GET .../flow?stage=` keep, of a read's shown nodes after the fold, those of one stage and the edges between them; gap rows, notes and every kept node stay as without it. Plugin 0.30.0, a MINOR for the key `get_flow` takes.
- **Measured.** `specengine-eval scale` times the Top read, a group's, the widest stage read, `spec trace --summary` and the tree over a corpus with a flow shape: at 100k about 1 s each, the summary 0.54 s, linear; RM-0054's bars still hold for `show`, `--links`, `search` and `bundle`.

The Stage filter on the screen is 2b's (RM-0084).
