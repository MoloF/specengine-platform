# stream-life (Phase 7, step 11c-2): a hub's streams end after a maximum life

Split from RM-0065 on 2026-11-03 (the orchestrator's decision Q7 of `docs/features/hub-accounts.md`, the owner's review open). A stream passed the door once, so it outlives a disable: the UI's events stream (`tail.rs`, `docs/canon/hub.md` "Known limits") and `/mcp`'s GET stream (`docs/canon/hub-mcp.md` "Known limits"). On a hub alone, each ends after a maximum life of 300 s, and the client reconnects through the door, which refuses a disabled person. The decision is ADR `hub-accounts`, issued by RM-0065; this item builds it. 1-1.5 pipeline hours.

- **Done** (AC17 of `docs/features/hub-accounts.md`). On a hub, the events stream and `/mcp`'s GET stream end after an injected life, proved in units as a session's idle end is (`hub-mcp.md` "Sessions"). A reconnect of the events stream with `Last-Event-ID` loses no event; whether `/mcp`'s GET stream resumes is this slice's to settle. A local daemon's stream never ends. M: no cap; the cap on a local daemon too.
