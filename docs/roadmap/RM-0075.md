# stream-life (Phase 7, step 11c-2): a hub's streams end after a maximum life

Shipped 2026-11-03 (`docs/features/stream-life.md`, ADR-0068, `docs/canon/serve.md` "Live tail", `docs/canon/hub-mcp.md` "Sessions"). On a hub alone, the board's events tail and `/mcp`'s GET stream end 300 s after they open (`STREAM_LIFE`, a constant). They end between two events and send nothing at the end, and the session stays. The client reconnects through the door, which judges the person again, and loses no event: the tail resumes with `Last-Event-ID`, and nothing rides `/mcp`'s GET stream. A local daemon's streams never end for their age. The UI is unchanged. This was split from RM-0065 as Q7. The orchestrator decided it, and the owner's review is open.

Still open: SG1-SG2 at the gate, step 11b-3. One mutation survives, since a 300 s cap is not waited for (`docs/canon/serve.md` "Known limits").
