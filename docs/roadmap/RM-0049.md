# hub-identity (Phase 7, step 8): a public origin and one trusted login, nothing that confirms

Hub mode of `specengine-http`, entered by one flag, `--public-origin`; `spec serve` gains nothing and a local daemon stays as it is. A second fence compares the public origin, demands one proxy-set `Remote-User` on every request, records that login as the actor of five plain card writes (`card_new`, `card_move`, `card_edit`, `card_assign`, `card_cancel`), serves `GET /api/identity` and refuses whatever needs consent until `hub-confirm`. The "Acting as" box goes read-only on a hub. Issues ADR-0058 `hub-mode`, which reverses the single-user premise on the owner's word of 2026-11-01, and ADR-0059 `hub-identity`, which supplements ADR-0034 and ADR-0057: no account, token or session inside SpecEngine, the daemon trusting that one header, and no agent running on that host. A request with no identity is refused on every route, and two identities give two `as` values. `[team]`, two `Decided-by` values and a person in `Author` moved to RM-0050. Spec: `docs/features/hub-identity.md`. 3.5-4.5 pipeline hours.
