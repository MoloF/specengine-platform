# Load flakes: two CLI tests that fail under full parallel load

`failing_test_removes_evidence` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/verify_tests_lock.rs` sets `[verify] timeout = 1` for its time-out half, so its nested test runs have 1 s each and can hit it under full parallel load. Seen once in a full run on 2026-10-30; 3 of 3 passes alone.

`a_fifo_swapped_in_after_the_size_check_never_holds_the_open` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/round.rs` needs both outcomes of a swap race. Seen once under full load; it passed in two other full runs and 6 of 6 alone. Not drafted.
