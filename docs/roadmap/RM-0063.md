# Load flakes: tests that fail under full parallel load

`failing_test_removes_evidence` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/verify_tests_lock.rs` sets `[verify] timeout = 1` for its time-out half, so its nested test runs have 1 s each and can hit it under full parallel load. Seen once in a full run on 2026-10-30; 3 of 3 passes alone.

`a_fifo_swapped_in_after_the_size_check_never_holds_the_open` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/round.rs` needs both outcomes of a swap race. Seen once under full load; it passed in two other full runs and 6 of 6 alone.

`gate_wrapper::a_stalled_spec_is_2_within_the_limit` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/gate_wrapper.rs` took 7.69 s against its 7.5 s limit once in a parallel run; 4 of 4 pass alone, at about 7.2 s. It recurred 0 of 1 under load: 7.218 s in a later full run, 3 of 3 alone. It failed again in a full workspace run during `hub-confirm`, `pipes closed late: 7.665s`; 3 of 3 alone. Not drafted.

`gate_wrapper::a_stopped_hook_leaves_nothing_behind` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/gate_wrapper.rs` failed twice under parallel load on 2026-10-31: a `sleep` child outlived its 1.1 s budget while `spec` and the watchdog had gone. 6 of 6 pass alone; that slice touched no hook.

`concurrent_raises_store_one_item` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/drift_dedup.rs` and `new_until_kills_a_slow_git_at_the_deadline` in `crates/specengine-store/tests/worktree_git.rs` are load-sensitive: each seen once, not reproduced.

`ac11_no_spec_file_is_read_and_the_index_is_untouched` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/gate.rs` failed once in a full workspace run during `hub-confirm` with an empty first document; 3 of 3 alone.

`a_large_write_is_read_to_its_end_and_decided` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/gate.rs` is a wall-clock bound on a 30 MB input; it passed 8 of 8 alone and in two full runs.

`spec gate`'s 3 s deadline (`GATE_DEADLINE`) answers `unavailable` under CPU starvation, so the tests comparing gate answers, `gate_answer` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/cards.rs` and `card_trace.rs`, now ask up to 5 times.

`d4_two_simultaneous_starts_leave_exactly_one_listening` in `crates/specengine-http/tests/serve.rs` failed once with `no listening line within 60s`; clean in 8 solo runs.

`a1_a_bad_team_leaves_show_card_and_gate_as_they_were` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/team_config.rs` failed once under load with `spec gate` exit 2 and empty stdout; not reproduced in 12 repeats, likely `GATE_DEADLINE`'s 3 s.

`unfinished_predecessor_never_holds_a_task` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/task_next_roadmap.rs` failed once with `no verdict (no answer within 3 s)`, likely the same deadline.

Seen again under full load during `hub-deploy` (2026-11-03), each clean alone: `a_fifo_swapped_in_after_the_size_check_never_holds_the_open` in `round.rs` (3 of 3 alone), and `a_large_write_is_read_to_its_end_and_decided` and `ac05_the_path_is_normalised_before_the_zone_match` in `crates/specengine-cli/tests/gate.rs`.

Deferred to the next release, `v4`, on 2026-11-04: the owner's instruction to do only what the hub's release to a server needs (08 s2 Phase 7, "Release scope").
