# hub-ops (Phase 7, step 11b-2): off-host dumps, restore, upgrade, health and turning on `--push`

Shipped 2026-11-04 (`docs/features/hub-ops.md`, `docs/canon/hub-ops.md`, ADR-0071), the last code item of release `v3`. Restic as the destination (Q5), S1-S12 and the decisions at implementation and review are the orchestrator's working answers and await the owner's review.

- **Dumps.** Every 15 minutes, on a UTC grid, two sidecars with no network stage on tmpfs each served project's queue (`spec export state`) and the door's state (its users file, Authelia's passkey and TOTP exports, the MCP users file, the act log, under `hub-admin`'s lock); `backup`, on restic's image, alone with a network, sends them encrypted to any backend restic reaches, set in `secrets/restic_env`. The deploy key and Authelia's secrets never leave the host.
- **Restore, upgrade, `--push`.** Runbooks in the canon: into an empty door (`hub-admin restore`) and empty queues, the floor raised after `--push`; an upgrade after a dump, back by it; a write key and the forge's rules lifted for it, after the gate.
- **Health.** `specengine-health`: the fence's loopback 403 and port 443; `backup`'s `fresh`: a whole round and both parts within 35 minutes.
- **Next.** The owner's gate 11b-3 on a VPS: tag `v0.29.1`, then VF4-VF9, HO1-HO17, H1-H17, G1-G10 and the claude.ai check, then `--push` and real people.
