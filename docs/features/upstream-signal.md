# upstream-signal: a subtask sees what changed above it, on the CLI, over MCP and on the Board (RM-0105)

## Why

The writer corrects a parent task's goal, or a decision lands on its document, and a subtask in another department saw nothing: a mark covers only a card's direct targets (`docs/canon/spec-trace.md` "Suspect"), a split's subtask targets only its kind's nodes under the parent's (`docs/canon/card-split.md` "The rule"), and no card write touches another (ADR-0074).

Now an open subtask carries `upstream`, what changed above it: its parent's goal, criteria or targets edited, a question, discrepancy or decision applied on their nodes, the parent reading suspect or correcting. `spec card show --marks` and `get_card` list the changes, `spec card list --marks` and `list_cards` flag the row, the Board's tile says "Changed above" and the work item lists the lines. Computed per read from the queue's events and the index, never stored; it moves, refuses and gates nothing (ADR-0012). No ADR: it rests on ADR-0074, ADR-0012 and ADR-0077, as its own key.

## Description and interactions

Each decision is canon now, in `docs/canon/card-upstream.md`: D1 its own key, D2 three sources, the direct parent, open subtasks, D3 by `seq` and how it ends, D6 the cap of 16: "The rule"; D4 riding `--marks`, D5 one uncapped read per call: "Reads"; D7 plugin 0.41.0: "MCP, daemon, hub, plugin"; D8 the tile's badge and the work item's part: "Screen".

The owner's questions, open, their working answers built: **Q1** its own key, never inside `mark`; **Q2** the parent's goal, criteria or targets edits, applied deciding items on the nodes and the parent's mark, by `seq`; **Q3** the direct parent, open subtasks, the queue the read runs in; **Q4** it ends on accept, cancel or a flow-down edit, no acknowledge act; **Q5** "the parent's document changed" shown, worded as the parent's mark. The writer's W1 to W7 (the child's own items aside, the mark line's end, the anchor's move, a name never an email, the card route without marks, `on` the first meeting node, current targets) are in that canon too.

## Rules and edge cases

In the canon: `card-upstream.md` "The rule", "Known limits". **Rollout**: an older `specengine-mcp` or daemon serves no `upstream`; no schema step, backup format, config key, route or tool; a hub takes it at its next upgrade.
