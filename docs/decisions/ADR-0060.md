# ADR-0060

**Decision.** Supplements ADR-0057 and ADR-0059: on a hub, accept, clear, group clear, stages and round answers open to the logins a project's `[team]` in `specengine.toml` names, read per request, and no spec file changes. Three roles, group clear above `member`, a required `email`, a session-level second factor at the proxy. The fence sorts routes into classes; a terminal is never judged.

**Why.** Zone leads and reviewers need to act with no terminal, and who may is a reviewed commit, not a second truth on a host. Read by these acts alone, a bad `[team]` stops no read or plain write (ADR-0012).

**Cost.** A forged `Remote-User` now accepts. Older binaries refuse `[team]`. An assignee can drop themself or reassign and name themself reviewer, each step recorded under a login. `by` and assignee are free text, so a display name in `assignee` does not trip "never its assignee". A removed login's open browser keeps reading what it has. The step-up is session-level: a second factor on the session, not a person behind each click.
