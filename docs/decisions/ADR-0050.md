# ADR-0050

**Decision.** `spec lock rebase`, on the owner's terminal after one `[y/N]`, rewrites `spec.lock` into this build's `format` and `code_recipe`: each entry's code hashes re-derived from its own `file_blob`, never from `HEAD`'s tree or the working tree; key, `rev`, `spec_hash`, `file_blob`, `commit`, `verified_*` kept; an entry it cannot re-derive dropped and named, never guessed. One commit of the lock alone, `Lock-mode: rebase`, verified as an accept's. Never across `norm_recipe`, into an older format, nor by MCP, a role, a hook, CI or the daemon.

**Why.** A grammar bump or a new format leaves every lock unreadable and no writer may carry it over (ADR-0041); the accepted blobs still hold what was verified: a rebase changes how it is recorded, not what.

**Cost.** Amends ADR-0041 (a writer beside accept and regen, hashing accepted blobs, never the current tree) and ADR-0042's "until re-accepted" (until rebased). A blob lost to gc drops its entry to `unlocked`; a format-1 entry's levels come from its blob's markers. Adds to ADR-0018; supersedes nothing.
