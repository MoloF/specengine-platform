# ADR-0089

**Decision.** A reference's file enters a repository only through a proposal of kind `reference`, applied by `apply_proposal` once a person confirms. One commit with provenance writes bytes BLAKE3-equal to the reference's at the home a project's `[references] homes` names by the document's kind, beside the document, and adds one typed, uncounted entry `<path> | <what matters>` to its `references:` key: no revision raise, no drift. A home `.gitattributes` gives to LFS gets an LFS pointer, never the raw blob; a hub's image ships git-lfs, configured as no filter, and skips the smudge. Every card targeting the document inherits its entries. `[auto]` never applies the kind. Narrows ADR-0088's "never a repository".

**Why.** Approved material belongs in canon beside its entity, through the one writing door, without swelling git with raw media.

**Cost.** Queue schema 17 and dump format 10 (`ref_id`), index format 22: every binary and the hub move together, a dump first. git-lfs joins the image. A confirm copies up to 256 MiB inside the project's turn. A promoted file lives twice on a hub.
