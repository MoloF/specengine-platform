# ADR-0071

**Decision.** Accepted by the orchestrator in the owner's absence, awaiting the owner's review. Supplements ADR-0067 and ADR-0068. Every 15 minutes two sidecars with no network stage on tmpfs each served project's queue (`spec export state`) and the door's state: its users file, Authelia's own passkey and TOTP exports, the MCP users file and the act log, under `hub-admin`'s lock. Restic sends them, encrypted on the host, to the operator's backend; only its sidecar has a network. The deploy key and Authelia's storage key never leave the host: a restore imports under new ones. No crate changes.

**Why.** Losing the VPS must not lose the queue or force a re-enrolment. A copy of a running door's SQLite is unsafe, and no pinned image has `sqlite3`; the door's exports do not depend on its key. Restic encrypts client-side and reaches most backends with no code.

**Cost.** An outside storage account. The restic password and `restic_env` must be kept off the host, or the dumps cannot be read. About 20 minutes of work die with a host. An intruder on the host can delete the backups unless the backend locks objects. Three more services.
