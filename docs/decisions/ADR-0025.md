# ADR-0025

**Decision.** SpecEngine is released under the MIT license, copyright (c) 2026 MoloF (`LICENSE`, `license = "MIT"` in the workspace).

**Cost.** No explicit patent grant and no mandatory visible credit beyond the notice (Apache-2.0 would add both).
