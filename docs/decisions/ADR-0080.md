# ADR-0080

**Decision.** The whole-corpus map lays out with `d3-force` 3.0.0, typed by `@types/d3-force`, at exact pins. It runs a fixed tick count from d3's seeded start, then draws on one 2D canvas. Both live only in `ui/src/map/`, a lazy chunk, under the canvas rules: read-only, the wheel scrolls the page, zoom by key, a List twin. This amends ADR-0033: the allowlist grows from 19 to 21. ADR-0011's stack holds.

**Why.** The owner chose the whole corpus. `@xyflow/react` is placed by hand and keeps a DOM node per box, too slow past a few hundred. A force layout needs an engine. `d3-force` is about 6 kB gzip, ISC and seeded since v3, and two of its three dependencies are already in the lockfile. `sigma` with `graphology` costs about 45 kB gzip, draws through WebGL, which jsdom lacks, and zooms by wheel.

**Cost.** Two pins. Drawing, hit testing and focus are written by hand. Assistive technology cannot read a canvas, so the List carries the meaning. Past 5 000 drawn nodes, only the most used are shown.
