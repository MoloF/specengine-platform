# ADR-0079

**Decision.** Supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0077. A card may hold a mandate, `binding` or `open`, named per subtask by the asking side when it creates a card with a reviewer; only that reviewer changes it. Binding: a departure goes back as a return with a question. Open: the department decides and says why; the parent shows its subtasks' decisions. The mandate only orders the page's acts and frames its dialogs: no move, accept, rollup, stage, approve, confirm or gate reads it, and a binding task's decision stays recordable (ADR-0012).

**Why.** The owner's workflow: no shifting of responsibility. A task that goes down is binding, or its department decides, and the writer sees what it decided.

**Cost.** Queue schema 12 and backup format 6: older binaries refuse the queue, and the hook gives no verdict until reinstall; the hub upgrades with a dump first. The reviewer compare trusts a typed name on a terminal. A card read scans its subtasks' items.
