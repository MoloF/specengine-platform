# ADR-0078

**Decision.** Supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0074 and ADR-0076. A split is one act: a parent task, new on a document or one that exists, and a subtask per chosen discipline, each a hand-off with the parent's reviewer, placed by the new-card rule; one transaction, all cards or none. It writes no stored card, so no other card write creates, moves or refuses another (ADR-0074). `[[card.split]]` rows under `[card]` name a kind and disciplines: they pre-select and pick a subtask's targets, and never refuse, require or gate (ADR-0012).

**Why.** The owner's workflow: a writer's document goes to every department it needs in one act, not a New task and then a Hand off per department.

**Cost.** A `[card]` holding `split` fails an older binary's card surfaces until every terminal, plugin and hub upgrades. No dedup: two splits make two sets. A split names cards of one store.
