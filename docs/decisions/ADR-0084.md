# ADR-0084

**Decision.** A live document whose typed `disciplines:` names card disciplines is a practice, and its `{#ID}` sections are its rules. A card's context and a `T-` task's package, through the cards that list the task, carry the practices of their disciplines. They go in the bundle's last layer, within `bundle_task`. A rule that does not fit is named in the not-included list. A practice gates, refuses and judges nothing.

This extends ADR-0027, and amends the bundle's rule: candidates come by link type, direction and `[ids]` scope, and in a card's or task's context also by discipline, never by kind.

**Why.** A department's agreed rules should reach the work they govern, so an agent cites a current rule instead of keeping it in mind.

**Cost.** A second selector in the bundle besides links; a long rulebook shrinks to IDs that must be fetched; a practice changes only by proposal; `INDEX_FORMAT` and the plugin each take a step.
