# ADR-0074

**Decision.** Supplements ADR-0056. A hand-off is a child card in the receiving trade's flow, its reviewer the asking side, named at creation; never a move across flows. The receiver takes it with a move; "accept" stays the asking side's word and act. A return is a move into the flow's one configured `return` column, with a required reason kept on the move's event; only the child moves (Q1). No card write moves, creates or refuses another card.

**Why.** Trades hand work across flows and send it back with a reason, and ADR-0056's accept must keep its meaning. A rule met in the same act blocks nothing (ADR-0012); the core names no column (ADR-0008).

**Cost.** Older binaries refuse the `return` key. A reason survives only in a card's last 64 events. Nothing follows a parent's children: its owner reads the rollup. On a hub, accepting a hand-off's result needs a `[team]` login.
