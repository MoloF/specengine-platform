# ADR-0077

**Decision.** Supplements ADR-0056, ADR-0057, ADR-0074 and ADR-0075. A question, discrepancy or decision may name one card of its own queue, set when raised, never changed; a decision may say why. Its record names no card: the card reads its items back. The page raises a question or decision on a card through the card door, under the acting name, a plain write on a hub. An answer is a stage on the item, confirmed as any other (ADR-0035, ADR-0061). No card write, gate or decision reads the link (ADR-0012).

**Why.** The owner's pipeline: a task goes back with a question, the answer becomes a recorded decision, and who decided what, and why, stays visible (Q5, Q11).

**Cost.** Queue schema 11 and backup format 5: older binaries refuse the queue, the hook gives no verdict until reinstall. The daemon raises proposals beyond `/mcp`, locally under a name nothing verifies. A `{{why}}` template fails older approves. A card ID names a card of one store.
