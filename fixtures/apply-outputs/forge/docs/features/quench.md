# Quench {#MEC-QUENCH}

Quenching sets the blade's temper.

## Oil or water {#RULE-QUENCH-MEDIUM}

Oil gives a tough temper; water gives a hard one and may crack the blade.

## A cracked blade {#EDGE-QUENCH-CRACK}

A blade quenched in water at full glow cracks one time in five.
