# Firing

A firing ramps the kiln up to the peak, holds it there, then cools it.

## Ramp {#KILN-RAMP}

The temperature rises by the ramp rate each hour and never passes the peak.

## Hold {#KILN-HOLD}

At the peak the kiln keeps its temperature until the hold time ends.

## Cool {#KILN-COOL rev=2}

The kiln cools by the cooling rate each hour and stops at zero.
