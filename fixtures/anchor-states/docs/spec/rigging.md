# Rigging

What holds a kite to the ground: its rig, the gauge read beside it and
the tether.

## Rig {#KITE-RIG rev=1}

A rig's lines and span set its reach.

## Gauge {#KITE-GAUGE rev=1}

A gauge reads the wind low and high.

## Tether {#KITE-TETHER rev=1}

The tether's tension is bound by its place only.
