# Kite flight

A kite rises on the wind, held by its line; every rule below is one
system of the game.

## Steady {#KITE-STEADY rev=1}

A kite held still keeps its height.

## Lift {#KITE-RENAME rev=1}

Lift grows with the wind's square and the kite's area.

## Drag {#KITE-CARRY rev=1}

Drag pulls the kite downwind.

## Tail {#KITE-SKETCH rev=1}

The tail's angle steadies the kite.

## Gust peak {#KITE-STRAY rev=1}

A gust peaks above the mean wind.

## Trim {#KITE-TRIM rev=1}

Trim balances lift against drag.

## Pull {#KITE-PAIR rev=1}

The line pulls north and south alike.

## Gusts {#KITE-CLONE rev=1}

Gusts from either side are computed alike.

## Reel {#KITE-CONTEST rev=1}

The reel takes line in at one rate.

## Spin {#KITE-MARK rev=1}

A spinning kite slows over time.

## Wind cap {#KITE-WIND rev=1}

The tuned gust cap bounds every gust.

## Banner {#KITE-BANNER rev=1}

A banner hangs from the line.

## Route {#KITE-TOOL rev=1}

The planner routes a flight and its rests.

## Reel speed {#KITE-LAG rev=1}

The reel's speed follows the line's length.

## Fog {#KITE-FOG rev=1}

Fog thins the light the kite is seen by.

## Echo {#KITE-DECOY rev=1}

An echo of the lift, never accepted.
