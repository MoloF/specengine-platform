# Ride fees

What a ride costs, from unlock to return (JRN-RETURN).

## Late return {#FEE-LATE-RETURN}

- The first 30 minutes of a ride are free.
- Every started quarter hour past them costs 1.00.
- A rider waiting at a full dock is not late while the dock shows no free slot.

## Day cap {#FEE-DAY-CAP}

- The fees of one rider in one day never exceed 12.00.
