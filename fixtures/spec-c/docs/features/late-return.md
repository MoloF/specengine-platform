# Late return

Charge the late-return fee of FEE-LATE-RETURN and keep the day cap of
FEE-DAY-CAP.

## Acceptance criteria {#acceptance}

### A 45-minute ride costs 1.00 {#CHK-01}

A ride unlocked at 09:00 and returned at 09:45 is charged one started quarter
hour past the free 30 minutes.

### The day cap holds {#CHK-02}

Fourteen late rides of one rider in one day are charged 12.00 in all.
