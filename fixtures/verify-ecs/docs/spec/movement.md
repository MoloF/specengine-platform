# Movement

A runner walks by default and sprints while stamina lasts.

## Sprint {#PERK-SPRINT}

Sprinting doubles the speed.

### Sprint cost {#PERK-SPRINT-COST}

Each second of sprinting costs the tuned amount of stamina.

## Walk {#PERK-WALK}

Walking costs nothing.
