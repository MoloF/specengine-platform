# Stamina

A runner spends stamina to sprint and regains it at rest.

## Regen {#PERK-REGEN rev=1}

Stamina regains a fixed amount per second while the runner rests.

## Drain {#PERK-DRAIN}

Sprinting drains stamina; at zero the runner walks.
