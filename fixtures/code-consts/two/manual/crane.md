# Crane

Rules of the harbour crane.

## Hook speed {#CR-0001}

The hook never moves faster than its rated speed.

## Wind stop {#CR-0002}

The crane stops when the wind passes the limit.

## Twin lift {#LD-0003}

Two cranes share one load only with a spotter.

## Floodlights {#CR-0007}

Floodlights are on whenever a crane moves after dusk.
